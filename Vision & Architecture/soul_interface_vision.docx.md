THE SOUL INTERFACE

                   A Living Map of Who You Actually Are

                            Vision & Architecture Document









The Core Idea

      "A game where your soul coordinates are the map."

The Soul Interface is not an astrology app. It is a personalized psychedelic journey —

an immersive, interactive world generated entirely from a user's birth chart data. No two

journeys are identical. The world you move through was shaped by where the cosmos

actually was the moment you arrived here.



This is built for the person who is lost and needs to find their way back. Not for someone

who wants to talk about their rising sign at brunch. For the person at 2am who feels

completely unmoored from themselves and needs something that actually meets them

at that depth.









What Makes It Different

The closest products that exist:

   •​ Co-Star — slick but superficial. Chart data rendered as clean UI. No depth, no

      world, no journey.

   •​ Meditation apps — atmospheric but generic. Not responsive to who you actually

      are.

   •​ AI reading tools — competent but just chat interfaces. No container, no

      experience.



The Soul Interface occupies an intersection nobody else is in: serious astrological

scholarship, genuine spiritual depth, immersive personalized world-building, and AI

synthesis across two complete traditions. Any one of those alone is interesting.

Together they are a completely different category.


The Three-Journey Architecture

The platform is structured around three distinct journeys, each a complete world, each

revealing a different layer of the same person.







Journey One — Tropical

Psychological, interior, Western in its landscape vocabulary. The forests, meadows, the

human emotional terrain. Greco-Roman mythological architecture. Light that feels like

consciousness examining itself. The Tropical seer speaks from Liz Greene, Howard

Sasportas, Noel Tyl, Alice Bailey — the soul-centered psychological tradition.



Journey Two — Vedic

Cosmic, vast, ceremonial. Planetarium quality, sacred geometry, mandalas forming and

dissolving. Temple structures, jungle, the cosmological scale of Jyotish. The sense of

something much older and larger than the individual. The Vedic seer speaks from

Parashara, Sri Aurobindo, Krishnamurti, Visti Larsen, Buddhist texts — the

liberation-oriented philosophical tradition.



Journey Three — Synthesis

Where the two visual languages bleed into each other. The forest has stars moving

through it. The temple is overgrown. Planetary positions create new geometries where

they touch. This is where the psychedelic quality peaks — you are seeing something

that has no single tradition as its home. The synthesis is the revelation: the thing that

neither tradition could show alone.



The synthesis journey is only accessible after traveling both traditions. That is not just

good spiritual design — it is good game design. Progression, revelation, reward.









The Seers

The seers are AI agents trained on curated book corpora — not on generic astrological

knowledge, but on specific texts chosen for specific reasons. They do not sound like a

model that knows astrology. They speak from the exact body of work they were trained

on.


The Tropical corpus draws from: Liz Greene, Alice Bailey, Howard Sasportas, Noel Tyl.

The Vedic corpus draws from: Sri Aurobindo, Krishnamurti, Satyananda Saraswati, Visti

Larsen, Buddhist philosophical texts.



Approximately 33,000 Tropical chunks and 35,000 Vedic chunks have been ingested

into ChromaDB collections. The infrastructure is built.



The seers also serve a second function: they are the consultants for building the world

logic itself. Rather than requiring the builder to master both traditions, the seers can be

queried directly — given a person with heavy Saturn and Ketu, what is the experiential

texture of their inner world? The seers answer from their corpora. That answer becomes

the visual and architectural logic of that user's specific journey.









The Visual World

The perspective is first-person fly-through — not a character walking through

something, but the user's own eyes moving through a world that is pulling them forward.

Like a rollercoaster. Like a lucid dream. The environment is the experience.



Visual reference points: What Dreams May Come, The Fountain, Dante's Inferno, Past

Lives, Future Lives.



The worlds include:

   •​ Cosmological planetarium environments

   •​ Ancient forests and deep jungle

   •​ Vast open plains and meadows

   •​ Mountain ranges and cave systems lit from within

   •​ Oceans, tidal and bioluminescent

   •​ Other worlds — volcanic, atmospheric, alien

   •​ Sacred geometry and temple architecture

   •​ Dark ceremonial underworld spaces



Crucially, these are not generic beautiful environments. Every landscape a person

moves through is theirs. Their chart is generating their world.



Chart-Responsive World Generation

The astrological positions determine the experiential texture of each environment:

   •​ Heavy water placements — oceanic, tidal, bioluminescent, emotionally vast


   •​ Fire dominant — volcanic, other worlds, burning skies, expansive

   •​ Earth dominant — ancient forests, mountain ranges, cave systems, enduring

   •​ Air dominant — high altitude, starfields, open plains, the planetarium quality



Planetary archetypes add further specificity: a Saturn transit through something vast,

cold, architectural, ancient. Venus through flowering meadows and warm light. Pluto

through the underworld, deep jungle, places where light barely reaches but something

luminous exists anyway.









The Experience

      "This is a literal game with a concept that is based off your soul

      coordinates that you navigate like a journey."

The Soul Interface is simultaneously: a game, a spiritual tool, a personalized

psychedelic experience, and a genuine astrological reading platform. These are not in

tension. They are the same thing expressed at different layers.



The psychedelic framing is precise, not just evocative. A psychedelic experience works

because set and setting prepare the nervous system to take something in differently

than it normally would. The immersive journey before the reading is the set and setting.

It is doing the real work of opening the person so they can actually receive something.



The seers are not the destination — they are mirrors. The user's chart, their question,

their current moment, is the actual material being worked with.









Technical Architecture



Current Infrastructure

   •​ ChromaDB collections: Tropical (~33K chunks) and Vedic (~35K chunks) fully

      ingested

   •​ RAG-based seer agents trained on curated corpora

   •​ Vibe coding approach using Claude Code for rapid iteration



Visual Build Stack


   •​ Three.js / WebGL — primary tool for immersive 3D environments, particle

      systems, atmospheric effects

   •​ Shader effects — distortion, color bleeding, sacred geometry animations

   •​ Spline — accessible visual editor for interactive animated scenes, exports to web

   •​ WebXR — browser-based VR as a future layer

   •​ Spline path camera — the fly-through rollercoaster perspective



Future Layers

   •​ Intake personalization — user birth data shapes which path they travel through

      each world

   •​ Transit-responsive environments — the world shifts as current planetary

      positions shift

   •​ Quest sequences and accumulated session memory

   •​ Past life regression journey architecture

   •​ VR headset experience









The Moat

The moat is not the tech stack. It is the coherence of the world.



The specific texts chosen for the corpora. The interpretive framework built across both

traditions. The understanding of how Tropical and Vedic speak to each other. The visual

language chosen for each planetary archetype. The three-journey revelation structure.

The decision that this is for the person who is lost, not the person who wants novelty.



A team of developers could not reverse-engineer this. The cosmological imagination

that produced it is not separable from the product itself. The moat is the singular vision.









     The vastness is not a problem to manage. It is the whole point.

Built to give people a place they can return to. A living map of themselves they can keep

                     navigating as they change and the transits shift.
